About GACEC

Building Djibouti's Future with World-Class Engineering

The Global African Company for Engineering and Construction has been established by a highly experienced staff with more than 20 years in the field of engineering and construction — covering all kinds of projects including buildings, roads, and water & sanitation systems.

The company holds complete capacity for planning, designing, engineering, investment, building, installation, and maintenance of various types of infrastructure and civil works across Djibouti.
